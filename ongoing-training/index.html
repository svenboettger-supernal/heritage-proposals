Enablement

Ongoing Training

Continuous Supernal-led training so the Heritage team can operate and refine each AI Employee on its own.

01 / Overview

The brief

Supernal trains the Heritage team alongside the build calendar. The goal is operational independence: by the end of each AI Employee's build window, the Heritage partners can manage the editorial CMS, review the help-desk knowledge base, and read the right operational signals without Supernal in the loop.

Training is light-touch and continuous, not a workshop series. Sessions are scheduled around live build milestones so coaching lands when the team is closest to the work.

02 / Objectives

What success looks like

  • Heritage partners can independently operate Foreman, Hank, Stephen, and Sam after their respective hypercare windows close.
  • Heritage editors can manage blog posts, team members, testimonials, and pricing tiers in the editorial CMS without engineering involvement.
  • Heritage's compliance officer can review conversation archives, audit trails, and retention rules without Supernal walkthroughs.
  • The partners can read the Monday rundown, account health, and prep-protocol cadence without coaching after week 8 of any AI Employee.

03 / Scope

What the training covers

3.1   Using each AI Employee

  • Foreman: capture meetings, approve action items, read the Monday rundown.
  • Hank: read the client record, approve drafts, follow the happy-path checklist.
  • Stephen: review and approve blog posts and the newsletter.
  • Sam: review escalations and step in when needed.

3.2   Reading the reports

  • Account health, content performance, help desk metrics, Monday rundown.
  • What each color and signal means. What to act on, what to ignore.

3.3   Editing and governance

  • Editing the websites in /admin: blog posts, team, testimonials, pricing tiers.
  • Compliance reviews of conversation archives.
  • Updating happy paths, escalation rules, and tone of voice as Heritage evolves.

3.4   How to think and act AI-native

  • Default to the AI first. Ask Foreman, Hank, or Sam before you ask a colleague.
  • Treat AI Employees as teammates. Give them feedback the way you would give it to a person.
  • Let the AI draft and do the work; you review and refine. Stop starting from a blank page.

04 / Cadence

How sessions run

Training happens inside three weekly meetings, plus async coaching in between.

  • Strategic, 30 minutes weekly: the Heritage partners and Supernal walk through what is coming, what just shipped, and how to read the platform's signals at a glance.
  • Project management, 30 minutes weekly: Heritage's PM leads and Supernal rehearse operating the platform together for the month ahead, with short demos of new capability.
  • End-user training, 1 hour weekly: the broader Heritage team using the platform gets walkthroughs of new features, Q&A, and hands-on practice.
  • Async question-level coaching in a dedicated Slack or MS Teams channel between sessions.
  • Recorded sessions and a written knowledge handoff at the close of each AI Employee’s hypercare window.
