Enablement · Across the partnership

Ongoing Training

Continuous Supernal-led training so the Heritage team can operate, refine, and extend each AI Employee on its own. Runs across the build calendar from June 2026 through December 2027.

01 / Overview

The brief

Supernal trains the Heritage team alongside the build calendar. The goal is operational independence: by the end of each AI Employee's build window, the named Heritage owner can adjust prompts, tune approval thresholds, manage the editorial CMS, review the help-desk knowledge base, and read the right operational signals without Supernal in the loop.

Training is light-touch and continuous, not a workshop series. Sessions are scheduled around live build milestones so coaching lands when the team is closest to the work.

02 / Objectives

What success looks like

  • Heritage's named product owners can independently operate Foreman, Wrangler, Press, and Marshal after their respective hypercare windows close.
  • Heritage editors can manage blog posts, team members, testimonials, and pricing tiers in the editorial CMS without engineering involvement.
  • Heritage's compliance officer can review conversation archives, audit trails, and retention rules without Supernal walkthroughs.
  • Tom Sr. and the partners can read the Monday rundown, account health, and prep-protocol cadence without coaching after week 8 of any AI Employee.

03 / Scope

What the training covers

3.1   Product development coaching

  • How Supernal builds AI Employees: the framework, the playbook, the trade-offs.
  • What “done” looks like for each phase, and how to read the Supernal weekly delivery note.
  • How to translate Heritage operational changes (new client phase, new outbound template, new partner firm) into AI Employee configuration without filing a change order.

3.2   AI Employee operation

  • Per-AIE handoff at end of hypercare: prompts, approval thresholds, escalation matrices, knowledge-base ownership, retention rules.
  • How to QA outbound drafts, evaluate conversation samples, and tune voice and tone.
  • How to read account health, ticket containment, and content performance reports and act on them.

3.3   Platform stewardship

  • Unified data layer: how Foreman, Wrangler, and Marshal share Tasks, Meetings, Documents, and Stakeholders.
  • Environment switcher and single sign-on management.
  • Adding new AI Employees in the future: what Supernal needs from Heritage to scope and quote a new SOW quickly.

04 / Cadence

How sessions run

  • One 60–90 minute live session every two weeks, scheduled with the named Heritage owner for whichever AI Employee is in build or hypercare.
  • A monthly 30-minute partner-level briefing (Tom Sr., Ryan) on platform state, upcoming changes, and decisions needed.
  • Async access to Supernal in a dedicated Slack or MS Teams channel for question-level coaching between sessions.
  • Recorded sessions and a written knowledge handoff at the close of each AI Employee’s hypercare window.
