CRM AI Employee · Sales · Heritage Strategies

Hank

Hank is Heritage's sales-side AI Employee. It is the single source of truth for every prospect and client, captures inbound leads, logs every email and meeting on the customer record, and answers questions by chat or voice.

01 / Overview

The brief

Delivery is staged. Stage 1 puts every interaction in one place and makes it queryable. Stage 2 turns that record into a daily rundown with account health and prepared outbound drafts. Stage 3 runs the prep protocol on cadence and connects CRM, Foreman, and Help Desk on one data layer, with a top-left environment switcher between the three.

Heritage owns the pipeline and approves every outbound message. The CRM AI handles capture, enrichment, logging, drafts, scheduling proposals, and next-best-action surfacing. No outbound to a client or prospect leaves the platform without a named approver.

02 / Objectives

What success looks like

  • Put every prospect and client interaction in one place. Replace the patchwork of Salesforce, Word docs, and sticky notes without forcing a tool migration on day one.
  • Give the partners one trustworthy way to ask about any client by chat or by voice, with sourced answers and named owners.
  • Cut manual follow-up work for Operations. Reminders and digests carry a next step, due date, and responsible party on every item.
  • Surface the next best action daily. Meetings overdue, documents expiring, dormant accounts, and expansion candidates each get a reason and an owner.
  • Produce on-brand outbound drafts that a Director approves in under five minutes per item. No autonomous outreach to HNW principals.

03 / Scope

Scope of work

Stage 1 · Weeks 0–6

Single source of customer truth

Logs every prospect and client touch in one place. Answers questions by chat or voice. Frees the team from chasing email threads and spreadsheets.

  • Inbound capture from Heritage Strategies and Heritage AI website forms, partner-firm referrals, EA inbox, and manual entry. Every capture creates a Prospect record with source, owner, and a confidence-scored profile enriched from public sources.
  • Email auto-logging through Outlook integration. Every email to or from a tracked stakeholder lands on the customer record with the next action flagged.
  • Per-customer view: stakeholder map, full timeline of meetings, emails, tasks, documents, and signed-off meeting letters. Deduplication runs against existing records before any new entry is created.
  • Conversational lookup by chat or voice with sourced answers. Sync layer reads from the existing Salesforce instance and active spreadsheets. No one has to migrate or change tools on day one.

Stage 2 · Weeks 6–10

Account health, daily rundown, and prepared outbound

Tells the partners every morning who is on track, who is at risk, and what to do today. Drafts the messages that need to go out.

  • Account health per Prospect and Customer: On Track, At Risk, or Critical. Each carries a plain-language reason: days in phase, overdue items, meeting cadence, or sentiment from notes.
  • Morning Digest of priorities and end-of-day digest of overdue items, sent to each Director and Point Person. Every item carries a next step, due date, and responsible party, in line with Heritage's communication standard.
  • Color-coded pipeline view (green, amber, red) with explicit reasons per account. The Monday rundown is built from these statuses so the meeting reviews exceptions, not the full list.
  • Revenue dashboard: pipeline value by phase, customer revenue by type, week-over-week delta, deal-velocity trend. Weekly opportunity digest covers warm prospects, expansion candidates, expiring documents, and dormant accounts to revive.
  • Outlook and email integration extends the Stage 1 logging into thread-level engagement signals: response latency, last reply, and re-engagement candidates feed the health score.
  • Outbound drafts (intro, follow-up, scope-of-work cover, meeting recap, NPS check-in) generated in Heritage voice. The Director approves before sending. Banned phrases and citation rules enforced from the locked style guide.
  • Pre-meeting prep summary delivered the day before: last interactions, open obligations, current scope-of-work state, recent vault changes. Materials ready before the meeting starts.
  • One-click meeting scheduling with calendar-aware proposed times. The EA confirms. The AI never books an HNW principal autonomously.
  • Stakeholder map per customer (principals, spouse, attorneys, accountants, partner advisors) populated from logged emails and meeting letters, with role, contact details, and last interaction.

Stage 3 · Weeks 10–14

Phase engine, protocol cadence, and unified platform

Runs Heritage's prep protocol on cadence and progresses every relationship to its next defined step. Owns the path from prospect to active customer to growth, on one shared data layer with Foreman and Sam.

  • Prospect phases (Approach, Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3) and customer phases (Active, Growth, Steady-State, Dormant). Entry and exit criteria configurable per phase. The AI proposes progression when criteria are met. The named owner confirms.
  • Auto-instantiated checklists on phase entry. Example: an Approach phase generates tasks to obtain a biography, add the prospect to the tracker, and schedule discovery. Tasks land in the Foreman workspace so Directors work in one place.
  • Prep protocol cadence: Pre-Internal, IR1, IR2, Partner Review, Client Meeting tracked per engagement. Slippage flagged with named owner and date before it reaches the partners.
  • After each client meeting, the signed-off meeting letter is the source of truth for what was agreed. Each action item becomes a task with owner and due date in the shared Foreman workspace.
  • Top-left environment switcher between CRM, Foreman, and Help Desk. Single sign-on. Single notification surface. Shared data layer for Tasks, Meetings, Documents, and Stakeholders modeled once.
  • Heritage AI platform integration. Book-production status visible per customer. Document inventory linked to tasks.
  • Help Desk integration. Tickets surfaced per customer with status and resolution history.

06 / Required inputs

What Heritage provides, by stage

Inputs are grouped by stage. Stage 1 inputs are required for launch in week 0 to 2. Stage 2 and Stage 3 inputs are required by week 6 and week 10 respectively. Approval delays extend cadence day for day. Several items may already exist within Supernal's stack from prior Heritage work. Supernal inventories what is reusable in week 0 and only asks Heritage to fill the gaps. Every input has a named owner and a committed due date in line with Heritage's communication standard.

6.1   Engagement and approvals

  • Single named approver for pipeline model, phase criteria, and outbound voice (recommended: COO). Decision on the existing Salesforce instance: keep as the Stage 1 sync source, archive, or retire.
  • Weekly 30-minute review call during build. Owner and time committed in week 0.

6.2   Stage 1 inputs (required for launch)

  • Active prospect list with source, current status, and assigned owner. Active customer list with owner, AUM range, and last interaction.
  • Stakeholder taxonomy: principal, spouse, attorney, accountant, partner advisor, other.
  • Heritage Strategies and Heritage AI website form endpoints with sample payloads.
  • Outlook and email read access for auto-logging. Microsoft 365 single sign-on.
  • Existing Salesforce instance access (read) for the Stage 1 sync layer. No migration required at launch.

6.3   Stage 2 inputs (required by week 6)

  • 5 to 10 examples of existing Heritage outbound copy: intro emails, follow-ups, scope-of-work covers.
  • Style guide: tone, do and don't, banned phrases, citation rules. Locked before drafts go live.
  • Approved disclosure copy for AI-drafted outbound. Approver list with sending authority per channel.
  • Send-on-behalf access via approver. ESP credentials and approved sender domain. Calendar booking tool admin access.

6.4   Stage 3 inputs (required by week 10)

  • Current prospect and customer phases with entry and exit criteria. Per-phase task list and ownership across Director, Point Person, EA, and Partner. Standard scope-of-work template. Customer-type segmentation: HNW family, partner-firm referral, board, other.
  • Heritage AI platform read-only API access for book status and document inventory. Help Desk AI Employee data layer read access for ticket surfacing. Existing scopes-of-work and meeting letters: one-time export for migration.
