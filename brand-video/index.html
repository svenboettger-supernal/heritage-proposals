Brand Video Bundle

Four videos shot together in June 2026 to anchor the visual identity across both Heritage Strategies and Heritage AI surfaces. Two product videos for the Heritage AI platform, one founder-narrated intro for the Heritage Strategies website, and one animation of the book manufacturing process.

01 / Overview

The brief

The bundle is built around a single shoot day in New York. By co-locating the production, the four videos share lighting, voice, and editorial tone. Costs land at roughly half what four separate shoots would carry.

Outputs feed every owned channel: the Heritage Strategies and Heritage AI websites, the email newsletter, the Heritage AI demo and pilot pipeline, and any partner-firm conversations that benefit from a short narrative aside.

02 / Objectives

What success looks like

  • Anchor the visual identity for both Heritage brands with one consistent production language.
  • Give Heritage AI two demo-ready product videos: one for end-book users and one for partner-firm brokers selling vault subscriptions.
  • Give Heritage Strategies a founder-narrated intro that sits at the top of the homepage.
  • Explain the Heritage Book methodology in 60 seconds via animation, usable in pitch decks and on the platform.

03 / Deliverables

The four videos

Product video, end-book users

  • For HNW end clients receiving a Heritage Book
  • Walks through what to expect, how to read it, and how to navigate the digital vault
  • Customer interviews considered (e.g. Fred Dana in NY office)
  • For Heritage AI

Product video, firm users

  • For partner-firm brokers (Ridgeback pilot and onward) who introduce vault subscriptions to end clients
  • Frames the producer-side workflow: book request, document collection, vault delivery
  • For Heritage AI

Intro video, Heritage Strategies website

  • Founder-narrated introduction to Heritage Strategies for the marketing site hero
  • Cut from the same shoot day as the two product videos
  • For the Heritage Strategies Front-End Website

Animation, book manufacturing process

  • Short animated explainer of how a Heritage Book is built from input to delivery
  • Scope to be clarified with Ryan in week 0
  • Usable across both Heritage brands and pitch decks

04 / Production

How the shoot runs

  • Single shoot day in New York, during June 2026 (subject to Jacqui’s availability and Tom Sr.’s confirmation).
  • Pre-production: Ryan and Sven provide questions, guidance, and customer-interview shortlist by week 0.
  • Post-production handled by Jacqui’s team. Three rounds of revisions per video.
  • Final delivery: 1080p and 4K masters, vertical and 16:9 cuts where applicable, captioned versions for social and web autoplay.

05 / Required inputs

What Heritage provides in week 0

  • Confirmed engagement from Tom Sr. and Ryan.
  • Jacqui in the New York office during the agreed shoot week.
  • Customer interview shortlist (and consent) for the end-book user video.
  • Brand guidelines: colors, typography, voice and tone references.
  • Any existing footage or B-roll Heritage wants integrated.
  • Approved disclosure copy for any on-screen text or voice claims.
