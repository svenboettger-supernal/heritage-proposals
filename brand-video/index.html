Brand Video Bundle

Two ways to bring Heritage to life on camera. Option 1 is a set of great explainer videos. Option 2 is a professional commercial team on a full shoot. Watch an example of each below, then pick the one that fits.

01 / Overview

The brief

The videos anchor the visual identity across both Heritage surfaces. You choose the production approach that fits: a set of great explainer videos, or a professional commercial team on a full shoot.

Outputs feed every owned channel: the Heritage Strategies and Heritage AI websites, the email newsletter, the Heritage AI demo and pilot pipeline, and any partner-firm conversations that benefit from a short narrative aside.

02 / Objectives

What success looks like

  • Anchor the visual identity for both Heritage brands with one consistent production language.
  • Give Heritage AI demo-ready product videos for end-book users and for partner-firm brokers selling vault subscriptions.
  • Give Heritage Strategies a narrated intro that sits at the top of the homepage.
  • Explain the Heritage Book methodology in 60 seconds, usable in pitch decks and on the platform.

Choose your approach

04 / How it runs

How it runs

  • Option 2 is built around a single shoot day in New York, around June 2026 (subject to the production team's availability and the senior partner's confirmation). Option 1 needs no shoot day.

What Heritage provides
