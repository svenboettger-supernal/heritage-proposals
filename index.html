AI Employees

Four hires for the partnership

The team. Each AI Employee owns a domain end-to-end, ships work the team uses from day one, and operates inside named approval rules. Foreman joins Dave, the production AI Employee already running behind the Heritage Book and Digital Vault workflow.

Project Manager

Foreman

Heritage's shared workspace for everything in flight. Captures meetings, drafts post-meeting summaries, chases delegated items, and answers status questions by chat or voice. Three phases over six months.

CRM

Wrangler

Single source of truth for every prospect and client. Captures inbound, logs every email and meeting, drafts outbound, and surfaces the next best action daily. Sixteen weeks across three stages.

Webmaster

Press

Produces and ships ongoing marketing content across both Heritage Strategies and Heritage AI websites and the shared email newsletter, on a monthly cadence. Twelve weeks to steady state.

Help Desk

Marshal

In-app chat widget that handles inbound support across the platform. Answers producer and firm-admin questions, retrieves documents, opens tickets, and hands off cleanly to humans. Twelve weeks to autonomous.

Websites

Two front-ends, one playbook

Independent marketing sites for Heritage Strategies and Heritage AI, built on a shared component library and editorial CMS so Press can publish into both without rebuild work.

Marketing surface

Heritage Strategies Website

Marketing surface

Heritage AI Website
