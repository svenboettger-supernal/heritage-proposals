Secure Operations

Dedicated Private Cloud

Heritage's own isolated Google Cloud project, with the same frontier models served privately through Vertex AI under a Google BAA. Same models, same accuracy, full isolation. Operated as a managed service.

01 / Overview

The brief

Heritage data already runs in a Supernal-controlled cloud, on frontier models that never train on it. The dedicated private cloud moves Heritage into its own isolated Google Cloud project, with the same frontier models served privately through Vertex AI under a Google BAA. The models and accuracy do not change, only the isolation does.

Supernal provisions, runs, and maintains the environment as a managed service. Heritage gets a dedicated store, its own audit logs, and nothing shared with another client, with no change to the day-to-day experience for the team.

04 / Scope

What the build covers

  • A dedicated, isolated Google Cloud project for Heritage, with no compute, network, or storage shared with any other client.
  • The same frontier models served privately through Vertex AI, under a Google BAA, with data processed but not retained by the provider.
  • Migration of the Heritage workloads and the Heritage Vault into the dedicated project, with no change to models or accuracy.
  • A dedicated store with role-based access control and Heritage's own audit logs.
  • Operated as a managed service: Supernal provisions, monitors, patches, and maintains the environment.
