Website & Content AI Employee

Stephen

Stephen builds, runs, and keeps both Heritage sites current: the Heritage Strategies firm site and the Heritage AI platform site. He hosts and maintains both, and keeps content flowing through a lightweight CMS with AI Employee functionality, drawing from compliance-approved sources.

01 / Overview

The brief

Two sites, one AI Employee. Supernal builds both Heritage websites across the first three months, then runs them: hosting, maintenance, updates, and content kept current through a lightweight CMS with AI Employee functionality. Website creation, ongoing management, and content sit in one engagement rather than a build project plus a separate content retainer.

Published content is drawn only from pre-approved sources, so everything that goes live stays inside Heritage's compliance perimeter. Supernal manages the pipeline. Heritage keeps final approval. Section 04 sets out exactly where the regulatory lines sit.

02 / Objectives

What success looks like

  • Replace the legacy Heritage Strategies site and launch the Heritage AI platform site, both on one visual identity.
  • Keep both sites current and secure without consuming Heritage staff time.
  • Clean, well-structured pages with sound on-page SEO foundations.
  • Make it easy for visitors to get in touch, with simple contact and inquiry forms delivered to Heritage.
  • Keep every published word inside the approved, on-file content set.

04 / Legal & compliance

Legal and compliance limitations

Securities and investment advisory services are offered through M Holdings Securities, Inc., a registered broker-dealer and investment advisor, member FINRA/SIPC. Heritage Strategies is independently owned and operated and is a member of M Financial Group. Every public-facing marketing communication is a communication with the public under FINRA Rule 2210 and is subject to broker-dealer compliance review and recordkeeping before it is published. This service is built around that reality, not against it.

4.1   Supernal does not author or approve regulated content

Supernal builds and operates the sites and manages the content pipeline. Supernal does not write original investment, insurance, or estate-planning claims, and Supernal is not a compliance reviewer. Regulated content originates with Heritage or with a pre-approved source, never with Supernal.

4.2   Published content comes only from pre-approved sources

Two sources are in scope, and only these two:

  • M Financial Group's compliance-approved content library, delivered through Lawton Marketing Group (LMG), covering 13 planning categories and maintained centrally by M's marketing team. Supernal adapts and publishes from this library.
  • Material Heritage supplies that has already cleared its own broker-dealer and compliance review.

Nothing outside these two sources is published.

4.3   Heritage retains final approval and compliance sign-off

Every item is staged and queued for Heritage approval before it goes live. Approval authority, FINRA Rule 2210 review, and the associated books-and-records obligations stay with Heritage and its broker-dealer. Approval delays extend the publishing cadence day for day.

4.4   Recordkeeping

All published content and its approvals are logged and exportable, so Heritage and its broker-dealer can meet books-and-records and FINRA audit requirements from a single trail.

4.5   What is deferred

This AI Employee curates and publishes from approved sources. Autonomous drafting of original newsletters or articles is out of scope for now, deferred until the compliance controls around net-new generation are agreed with Heritage's broker-dealer.

4.6   On the M Financial member-firm website program

M Financial's website program offers LMG templates, a $3,000 website credit available through September 30, 2026, and $800 per year hosting. That program is a separate offering. Supernal builds a custom site rather than an LMG template, so Heritage does not read as templated alongside every other member firm. Heritage keeps access to M's compliance-approved content library as a content source either way, which is the part of the M program this service uses.
