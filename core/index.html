Empowering Heritage to Become AI Native

The core build plan: three AI Employees on the team, covering project management, the sales pipeline, and customer support.

Executive Summary

AI Native means Heritage runs day-to-day work through named AI Employees that each own a function end-to-end and ship the work the team would otherwise do manually. This plan brings three new AI Employees: project management, CRM, and help desk. They join Dave, the production AI Employee already running behind the Heritage Vault workflow. Both Heritage entities share access throughout.

Captures meetings, drafts summaries, chases delegated items, answers status by chat or voice.

AI Employees

Three new hires for the partnership

Each AI Employee owns a domain end-to-end, ships work the team uses from day one, and operates within a defined scope, autonomy level, and approval rules set by Heritage. The three new hires join Dave, the production AI Employee already running behind the Heritage Book and Digital Vault workflow.

Project Manager

Foreman

Heritage's shared workspace for everything in flight. Captures meetings, drafts post-meeting summaries, chases delegated items, and answers status questions by chat or voice.

CRM

Hank

Heritage's CRM and the foundation for scaling sales. Every prospect in one pipeline, tagged by acquisition channel, with stage, last activity, and next step in view, then carried across the full client lifecycle from won through onboarding and renewal. Collaborates directly with Foreman on a shared task and data layer.

Help Desk

Celeste

In-app chat widget that handles inbound support across the platform. Answers producer and firm-admin questions, retrieves documents, opens tickets, and hands off cleanly to humans.
